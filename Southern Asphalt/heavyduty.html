Contact the company today to learn more about Southern Asphalt's complete range of asphalt pavement service options or to receive a quote.


Drainage Installation

  Improper drainage for rainwater can accelerate water damage cracking in asphalt pavement.  Southern Asphalt Company works to eliminate surface and sub-surface drainage issues to allow proper outflow and increase the lifespan of your paved surfaces.

Milling

  When asphalt has degraded and worn out over time, the simplest and cheapest solution is often to mill the existing pavement down to the desired depth, then repave over of the new base.  Southern Asphalt uses this method to save its customer’s money, and because all waste material produced by this method is 100% recyclable.

Grading

  Southern Asphalt oversees site grading, wet utilities, finish grading and paving for commercial, subdivision and public works projects. Site grading refers to building compaction, distribution of raw land, applicable earthwork, and building pads. Site wet utilities includes installation of sewer, water, storm drain, raw water, and fire services. Finish grading includes laying and compaction of AB preparation for concrete work, and the raising of manholes and hydrants. Paving includes the paving work for small paving projects. Southern Asphalt ensures that all of these tasks are completed efficiently, effectively and conform to all necessary codes and specification.

  Southern Asphalt uses the advanced laser controlled excavation equipment to ensure proper slope and compaction on the project’s stone base.  The base is the foundation for any paving project and done properly will help ensure the longevity of the project.  Southern Asphalt can self-perform excavation, grading, and paving on any project.
