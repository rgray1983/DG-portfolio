Our Services

From QC inspection to returns processing, we provide turnkey logistics services.


E-commerce

Our cloud-based WMS system can provide you with a tailored stock management system. Our WMS is linked to UPS, FedEx and USPS to ensure accurate shipping info and streamlined label printing. We can also integrate with your website shopping cart to automatically download orders for picking and verify stock status for product availability. This results in efficient operations, reduced costs and accurate order fulfillment.


Amazon FBA Preparation Service

We are able to fulfill orders directly from a range of marketplaces including your Amazon orders. However, for any client wishing to use the FBA option then our in-house preparation services are key to a smooth integration into an Amazon depot and helps to maximise availability.


Retail Distribution

Whether it is retail distribution center shipment, shipment of store-direct quantities (SDQ), or drop-shipping direct-to-consumers, SunPak can help with the ever-changing landscape of retailer requirements and demands.

Manufacturing Support

Sub-Assembly
